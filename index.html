§ 3

Understanding the Risks Involved

This application acknowledges the following known risks:

  • Two strong personalities.
  • Two competitive people.
  • Two people who feel deeply.
  • Two people who protect themselves differently.
  • Two people who are capable of unintentionally hurting each other when overwhelmed.

Ego would be.

The objective would never be: “Who wins?”

Applicant firmly believes those are two very different things.

§ 4

Terms & Conditions of Potential Boyfriendship

If approved, applicant agrees to the following operating conditions:

Emotional Safety Clause

Vulnerabilities shared during moments of trust may never be weaponized later during conflict. Ever.

Communication Clause

Honest communication is required, especially when uncomfortable. “I’m fine” will be considered suspicious behavior.

Space & Regulation Clause

Either party may request space when emotionally overwhelmed without fear of abandonment, punishment, retaliation, or passive-aggressive behavior. However, reconnection and communication must eventually occur.

Applicant believes healthy relationships require both regulation and repair.

Affection Clause

Consistent affection, reassurance, quality time, and emotional presence are mandatory relationship requirements.

Conflict Clause

The goal during disagreements is not defeating each other. The goal is understanding each other.

First person to intentionally hurt the other person loses automatically. Winning an argument at the expense of the relationship will also be considered losing.

Assumption Clause

Neither party may assume malicious intent during moments of emotional flooding or fear. Clarification is encouraged before conclusions are reached.

Competitive Clause

Healthy competition is acceptable. Trash talk is permitted within reasonable limits. Rock-paper-scissors may be used for minor disputes.

§ 5

Conflict Resolution Protocol

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited during disagreements:

  • Personal attacks
  • Humiliation
  • Manipulation
  • Silent punishment
  • Threatening the relationship impulsively
  • Using insecurities as ammunition
  • Weaponizing vulnerability

Applicant strongly believes that if two people are brave enough to be emotionally vulnerable with each other, that vulnerability must become protected territory.

Not a weapon.

Both parties agree that emotional flooding is real and temporary.

A hard moment is not automatically a failed relationship.
